Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering

What is BSCpE?

Computer Engineering combines hardware (electronic circuits, chips, devices) and software (programming, apps, systems). You learn how to build, design, and fix both computer parts and the programs that run them.

What you will learn:

  • Computer programming and coding
  • Electronic circuits and digital systems
  • Computer networks and internet systems
  • Microprocessors and embedded systems
  • System design and analysis

Career paths: Computer Engineer, Software Developer, Systems Analyst, Network Engineer, Hardware Designer, IT Specialist, and more.
